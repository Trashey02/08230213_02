About Me

Hello! 👋 My name is Tashi Yangzom, and I am currently pursuing a Bachelor of Education (B.Ed) at Samtse College of Education. I am passionate about exploring and teaching ICT, and I enjoy discovering how modern tools like web design and digital learning platforms can make education more engaging.

I see education as more than transferring knowledge; it is about sparking curiosity, nurturing confidence, and guiding learners to grow for life. Beyond the classroom, I am passionate about web design, database systems, and programming.

Beyond academics, I enjoy engaging in team-driven projects, exploring cutting-edge technologies, and creating coding experiments that enhance both my technical expertise and interpersonal skills. My aspiration is to become an educator who seamlessly blends technology into teaching to make learning more inclusive and impactful.
